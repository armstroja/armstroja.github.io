Questions

Before you commit.

Is it actually comfortable, or just sculptural?

Both, on purpose. The shell is high-resilience foam tuned to the firmness of a good lounge chair, and the seat geometry follows standard ergonomic curves underneath the radical silhouette. People routinely sit through entire dinner parties in it.

How limited is "limited"?

400 pieces total — 100 per colorway — each numbered and signed by Studio Volta. When the edition sells out, the master mold is destroyed at a documented event. No reissues, no "second runs."

What does shipping cost, and how long does it take?

Shipping is included worldwide. Each chair is lacquered to order, so allow 6–8 weeks from order to delivery. You'll get the crate tracking link the day it leaves Lambrate, and import duties are pre-paid for the EU, UK, US, and Canada.

How do I clean and maintain it?

A soft cloth and warm water handle nearly everything — the flexible lacquer is sealed against coffee, wine, and fingerprints. Avoid solvents and direct radiator heat. A care kit with touch-up lacquer ships in the crate.

What if it doesn't work in my space?

You have 30 days from delivery to return it in the original crate for a full refund, including the deposit. We arrange the pickup. About 2% of chairs come back — usually swapped for a different colorway.
